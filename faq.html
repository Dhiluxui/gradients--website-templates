Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about asset formats, commercial licensing, the shader engine, and billing.

What is Grainient?

Grainient is an elite digital asset platform and shader engine designed for web developers, creative agencies, and UI/UX designers. It delivers over 1,000+ noisy gradients, smooth mesh blends, animated background loops, and a real-time in-browser shader synthesis tool.

Are client projects covered under the license?

Yes. Both the Pro Pass and Studio tiers come with a 100% royalty-free, perpetual commercial license. You can use any gradient, wallpaper, or synthesized shader in unlimited commercial client projects, SaaS products, mobile apps, and marketing materials.

How do I copy gradients into my codebase?

Simply click on any gradient in the gallery to open the Inspector Modal. You can copy clean standard CSS syntax (`background-image: radial-gradient(...)`), Tailwind CSS arbitrary utility classes, SVG filter data URLs, or a ready-to-paste React JSX component.

Do I need WebGL experience to use the Shader Tool?

Not at all. The Grainient Shader Tool runs mathematical wave harmonics client-side with a modern visual UI. You tweak color stops, speed, turbulence, and film grain with sliders, and the tool compiles the code for you in real time.

How often are new gradients released?

We drop new curated gradient packs and shader presets on a monthly basis. All Pro Pass and Studio users receive all future drops automatically for life at no extra charge.
